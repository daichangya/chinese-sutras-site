# jingxin-post-mvp-polish

## Why

MVP 工程清单已完成，但产品体验、语料质量、AI 可信度与专题深度仍明显低于原始规划与参考站水准。

## What

- Phase 1: Reader UX, home cards, verse share, chapter pagination
- Phase 2: Corpus preface stripping, MVP sutra list, colloquial pipeline
- Phase 3: Golden tests, daily refresh script, AI mock for CI/E2E
- Phase 4: Rich topic pages, local annotation highlights

## Scope

- No full CBETA 5000-sutra corpus in this change
- No user accounts; bookmarks/annotations stay client-side until later
